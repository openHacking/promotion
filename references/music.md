# Music, sound, and shared time

Choose music for the product and narrative, not only its energy. Verify license, source, creator, collection date, and asset hash. “Royalty-free” does not mean no copyright. The optional TinyWorlds track is CC0; its estimated 125 BPM does not establish an exact downbeat grid.

The original procedural score uses an authored beat grid and chord phrases. External sources support `sourceOffsetSeconds`; record the selected range, measured beat frames, and useful phrase frames in `timeline`. Listen before completing choreography.

`timeline.events` drives both visual action accents and SFX. A visual shot can reference the event by ID. Start motion early enough to land on the event. Use a few distinct transition, selection, resolution, and brand accents. Preserve quiet reading sections.

Mixing applies event-shaped music ducking, source trimming, a short entrance/exit fade, and a limiter. Verify the encoded soundtrack and actual visual contact frame. Technical duration and peaks are separate from a listening assessment.

For release, deliver only the final audible MP4 and soundtrack; internal silent video and music/SFX stems stay under `.internal/`.
