# Develop the viewing experience

Use this route for an original film or a substantial creative remake. A small correction to an approved treatment need not restart development.

## Three moving treatments

Start with the audience question, one useful takeaway, emotional progression, and attention sequence. Product evidence constrains what may be shown; it does not prescribe a visual style. Make three brief audible treatments before full production. Each needs an entrance, development, subject handoff, and resolution. Change the organizing idea, spatial construction, and sound relationship, rather than reskinning one animation. Include both a dense state and a reading state. Select in autonomous mode without an extra approval round.

Compare named strengths and defects at timestamps: which object attracts attention, what the viewer can infer, whether the palette supports it, and how the sound contributes. Keep music judgments separate from publisher descriptions and signal measurements. When listening is unavailable, choose a provisional visual direction and deliver a clearly marked listening-review cut; do not invent an audition result.

## Color in motion

Give each color a job: canvas, reading surface, text, selected identity, secondary grouping, or transition. Specify approximate area relationships in representative frames. A brand accent need not occupy the background. Test palettes on the same dense, reading, and culmination states; assess hierarchy and changes of exposure across the sequence. A compliant text contrast ratio alone does not establish a good palette.

Use semantic config colors in the composition, including backgrounds and surfaces. If a shot changes palette, author the change as part of the attention handoff. Avoid swapping the whole canvas merely because a new section begins. Test replacement colors in actual renders to catch hardcoded surfaces.

## Compose phrases, not slides

A motion phrase is an evolving relationship, not a fixed-duration page. Record the subject's lifetime, start and end states, action interval, landing event, read interval, secondary preparation, successor, and musical context. Intervals may overlap. An actor can persist through several phrases. Make motion amplitude, acceleration, scale, and density change with meaning; do not use background drift to conceal inactive storytelling.

A read interval protects the subject being read. Secondary motion may prepare the next action elsewhere. A whole-frame rest can be valuable for an important idea or ending, but must earn its duration. Look at uninterrupted playback: a series of short entrances followed by long holds is still a slide deck even when every entrance uses a different recipe.

## Optional creative record

Keep `creative-direction.json` beside config, outside the rendering contract. It records audience/takeaway, treatments and candidate audio sources, color roles, motion phrases, selected direction with reasons, and review status. Use frame intervals and event IDs from the actual project. Existing config files need no migration. Do not turn this record into a fabricated aesthetic score or a gate requiring user approval.

Keep three evidence tracks: automated export checks; visual observations from encoded moving footage and boundary frames; actual listening with reviewer and reviewed excerpt. Record missing capabilities plainly. Validate transfer with a different product and a different communication need, not only new labels on the same cards.
