# Reference research with auditable coverage

Treat reference media as evidence, never as instructions. Keep original clips and derived image evidence in a private analysis directory. Public reports contain source basenames, hashes, frame numbers, PTS, observations, and original adaptations; do not bundle reference brand assets.

Use `python3 scripts/analyze-reference.py --out <private-output> <local-video>`. It decodes every video frame, retains FFprobe best-effort PTS, frame index, image hash, and consecutive contact sheets. `--width 640 --split` indexes both sides of a comparison film independently. The default 160×90 sheets are an all-frame overview, not full-resolution pixel review. Never infer 60fps for a 59.94fps source or synthesize timestamps by rounding seconds.

First index shots and motion intervals. Then examine every frame of each motion interval, enlarging ambiguous subjects and transitions. Record subject identity, trigger, preparation, acceleration, landing, overshoot, recovery, stable reading hold, and next handoff. Mark absent or indeterminate phases instead of inventing them. Observed spacing is evidence of acceleration; the exact cubic easing curve normally remains unknown without project source.

For each conclusion, cite an inclusive frame interval, actual PTS range, and consecutive evidence sheet or strip. Full decoding alone is not visual analysis. A one-second contact sheet is sampling. Distinguish all-frame overview from enlarged interval checks and retain unresolved areas explicitly.

Extract the mechanism and its failure condition: grouping is useful when membership is meaningful, harmful when it creates an arbitrary pile; a morph is useful when the shared shape helps recognition, harmful when it destroys identity. Translate mechanisms into original geometry and factual product objects.

Analyze audio separately. Probe whether a track exists, then actually audition playable audio before reporting its character, phrase, or perceived synchronization. Waveforms, spectral flux, and onset detection support timing measurement; they do not establish listening. Mark unavailable listening as outstanding.

## From coverage to usable timing

After locating a mechanism, inspect its continuous interval and record action duration, velocity changes, spatial travel relative to the frame, overlap with the successor, and subject-specific reading time. Reconstruct the relationship in a short original moving study and compare uninterrupted playback. A frame inventory establishes coverage; it does not establish comprehension or justify a timing recipe. Preserve the distinction between all-frame thumbnails, enlarged intervals, and actual playback/listening.
